Help & Support

How do I transfer money?

Go to Transfer Money in the sidebar, fill in the recipient's details and amount, then confirm. You'll see a success toast once it's done.

How do I freeze my card?

Open Cards, and use the Freeze Card button under the card you want to lock. Unfreeze any time the same way.

Where can I download my statement?

Visit Analytics → Reports and choose Download statement CSV, or print directly from Transactions.

Is my data stored anywhere?

This is a demo dashboard. All data lives only in your browser's local storage — nothing is sent to a server.

Still stuck? This is a portfolio demo, so support is simulated — but in a real bank app, this is where a live-chat or call-back option would live.
